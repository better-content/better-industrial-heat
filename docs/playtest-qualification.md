## Shipped-source gameplay promise and acquisition

`HeatSyncMod.kt` registers industrial heat storage, pipes, coolant exchange and food thermal tracking; optional integrations are conditional on installed mods (Cold Sweat ambient/body temperature, Power Grid, PneumaticCraft, AE2), with a Create boiler-heater integration. `HeatSyncRegistries.kt` registers `better_industrial_heat:heat_pipe`, `:boiler_heater`, `:coolant_exchanger`, `:thermal_firebox`, creative hot/cold sources, and the bundled hot-water fluid and bucket. The shipped-source liquid-coolant definition is `src/main/resources/data/better_industrial_heat/liquid_coolants/water_to_hot_water.json`; `HeatSyncAe2Registries.kt` registers `:impossible_matter_transducer` **only** when AE2 is present. These are source capabilities, not proof that every integration works in the full pack or that players can acquire everything in survival.
